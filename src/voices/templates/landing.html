Chapter 02 — How it works

The right engine for every tongue.

No single model speaks everything well. So Nigtica doesn't ask one to — it routes your words to the engine that speaks them best.

01

Speak or type

Talk into your mic or tap a sample chip. No installs, no setup — it runs in the browser.

02

We route it right

Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa and Pidgin go to YarnGPT; English and foreign tongues go to Groq Whisper.

03

Hear & read

Play it back, download the audio, read the transcript — and find it all in your history.
