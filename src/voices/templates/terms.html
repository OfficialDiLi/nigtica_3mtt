Terms of Use

Last updated September 2026 — written plainly, on purpose.

1. What you're getting

Nigtica reads text out loud and turns your voice into text, in Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Pidgin, and English. The actual speaking and listening is done by our providers, YarnGPT and Groq — we're the friendly front door, they're the engine room.

2. The AI will get things wrong

Sometimes it mishears a name, mangles a number, or drops a Yoruba tone. Translations can lose the gist. If it matters — health, money, legal stuff, anything serious — check it with a human before acting on it.

3. Don't be a menace

Don't use Nigtica to harass, insult, or impersonate anyone. Don't make voices say things real people never said and pretend they did. And nothing illegal, obviously. Break these and your account goes away.

4. Your data

We keep your email, a scrambled password, and simple tallies (what you ran, when, how much). Your actual words and recordings? Never stored by us — though YarnGPT and Groq process them under their own rules. Want out? Hit Delete in the header and your account is gone; your history becomes anonymous numbers.

5. Play fair

There's an hourly limit so one person can't hog the service or burn through our API credits. Try to dodge it and we'll notice — the tallies in section 4 are how.

6. It's a demo

This is a challenge project, offered as-is. It might sleep, restart, or forget your account on the free hosting. No warranties, no promises — just our best effort.
